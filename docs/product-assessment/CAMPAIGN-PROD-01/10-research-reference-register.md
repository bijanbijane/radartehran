# 10 — Research & Standards Reference Register (`CAMPAIGN-PROD-01`)

- **Campaign ID**: `CAMPAIGN-PROD-01`
- **Purpose**: Connects every ergonomic, information-architecture, onboarding, and epistemic-trust decision in `CAMPAIGN-PROD-01` to foundational human-computer interaction (HCI) research and industry standards, explicitly noting both **Direct Applicability** and **Domain Limits** for the Iranian urban housing market.

---

## Foundational Research & Standards Mapping (`12` Core References)

| Ref # | Citation / Standard | Core Principle | Direct Application in Radar Tehran (`CAMPAIGN-PROD-01`) | Boundary / Limitation in Iranian Real Estate Context |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **R-01** | **W3C WCAG 2.2** (2023) — *SC 2.5.7 Dragging Movements & SC 2.5.8 Target Size (Minimum)* | All functionality using dragging must have a single-pointer non-drag alternative (SC 2.5.7); interactive targets must be at least `24×24 CSS px` (SC 2.5.8 AA). | Remedying the `53` elements `< 24×24px` in `ProductGlobalHeader`/`AreaUnitToggle` and ensuring the 3-state bottom sheet works via both pointer drag and single-tap buttons. | `24×24px` is only a minimum floor; on mobile transit/in-field usage in Tehran, primary controls require `44×44px` to prevent mis-taps. |
| **R-02** | **Apple Human Interface Guidelines (HIG)** — *Sheets & iOS Touch Ergonomics (`44×44pt`)* | Modal and non-modal bottom sheets should support standard detents (`collapsed`, `medium/peek`, `large`) with a clear grabber and `44×44pt` minimum hit targets. | Defines the 3-state snap-point geometry (`collapsed: 72px`, `peek: 40dvh`, `expanded: 86dvh`) so users can compare Punak's price card (`peek`) while still seeing map polygons. | Web browsers inside mobile viewports have browser toolbars (`bottom` URL bar on iOS Safari), requiring `dvh` and `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)` rather than native UIKit points. |
| **R-03** | **Google Material Design 3** — *Bottom Sheets, Navigation Bar (`3–5` destinations) & Touch Targets (`48×48dp`)* | Bottom navigation bars must contain **3 to 5 destinations maximum**; placing 6–7 items causes label truncation and mis-taps. | Directly justifies refactoring `RadarBottomNav.tsx` from `grid-cols-7` (`45.7px` wide on `320px`) to **5 columns** (`خانه`, `نقشه`, `کشف محله`, `نبض بازار`, `بیشتر`). | Power users still need fast access to `ابزارها` and `فضای من`, so the `بیشتر` (`More`) sheet must open in `< 150ms` with 1 tap. |
| **R-04** | **Steven Hoober** (2013, 2017) — *Design for Fingers, Touch, and People (Thumb Zone Ergonomics)* | `49%–75%` of mobile users interact one-handed; the top `150px` of a mobile screen is the hardest "stretch/ow" zone, while the bottom-center is the natural thumb arc. | Explains why placing duplicate search/budget controls in `FloatingTopBar` (`y: 80..170px`) and requiring top-of-screen taps (`y: 130px`) to close the `78vh` sheet causes high physical strain. | Map panning naturally occurs in the center of the screen; therefore, the middle `60%–74%` of the viewport must remain free of floating banners (`MapLegend`, `DevDiagnosticsHud`). |
| **R-05** | **Fitts's Law** (Paul Fitts, 1954; MacKenzie, 1992) — $MT = a + b \log_2(2D/W)$ | Movement time ($MT$) increases logarithmically with distance ($D$) and decreases with target width/height ($W$). | Expanding the bottom sheet drag handle hit zone from `20px` to `44px` and consolidating `18px`-tall header language/unit pills into a `44×44px` Display Settings Popover cuts acquisition time and error rate by `>50%`. | Increasing target size directly on the map canvas would occlude polygons; hence secondary controls must be grouped into popovers/sheets rather than enlarged inline. |
| **R-06** | **Hick-Hyman Law** (Hick, 1952; Hyman, 1953) — $RT = a + b \log_2(n + 1)$ | Decision reaction time ($RT$) increases logarithmically with the number of simultaneous choices ($n$). | Reducing simultaneous Inspector navigation choices from `5` top tabs + `4` Stage-2 domains + `12` Stage-2 sub-tabs + `6` global CTAs (`27` competing interactive switches) to **4 primary tabs** with Stage-2 collapsed by default. | Real estate buyers genuinely need deep verification data before committing billions of Tomans; progressive disclosure preserves depth without simultaneous choice paralysis. |
| **R-07** | **John Sweller** (1988) — *Cognitive Load Theory (Intrinsic, Extraneous, and Germane Load)* | Working memory is severely bounded; extraneous cognitive load (redundant UI controls, conflicting modals, visual noise) starves germane load (actual financial decision-making). | Unifying the **4 competing budget modals** (`OnboardingWizardModal`, `reverse_budget`, `FloatingTopBar`, `SmartSuggestModal`) eliminates extraneous load so first-time buyers can focus on cash + loan + *rahn* trade-offs. | Housing finance in Iran (combining `نقدینگی`, `وام مسکن اوراق`, and `رهن مستأجر`) has high *intrinsic* complexity that cannot be oversimplified into a single cash number. |
| **R-08** | **Pirolli & Card** (1999) — *Information Foraging Theory & Information Scent* | Users navigate information spaces by following "information scent" (cues predicting the value of deeper content relative to interaction cost). | Moving the **2-Column Buy/Rent Price Card and 1-Line Budget Fit Verdict above the fold (`Tier 1`)** gives immediate high-scent feedback when tapping a neighborhood, before asking the user to scroll through 11 analytical cards. | If `Stage2MasterWidget` is collapsed by default, its `<summary>` label must carry strong information scent (`"تحلیل پیشرفته: ماتریس سناریوها، هزینه محضر، استرس کلان و اصالت داده"`). |
| **R-09** | **Nielsen Norman Group (NN/g)** — *Progressive Disclosure & Mobile Bottom Sheet Guidelines* | Show only primary decision attributes initially (`Tier 1`); defer secondary (`Tier 2`) and expert (`Tier 3`) attributes to user-initiated expansion. | Structures the refactored `NeighborhoodInspectorDrawer` into 3 explicit tiers and relocates `QuickHoodRankingStrip` out of the global drawer header. | Over-nesting (> 2 levels of accordions) harms discoverability; therefore, `Tier 3` (`Stage2MasterWidget`) opens automatically whenever `uiMode === 'pro'` is selected. |
| **R-10** | **Edward Tufte** (2001) — *The Visual Display of Quantitative Information (Data-Ink Ratio)* | Maximize the proportion of ink devoted to non-redundant display of core data; eliminate administrative chartjunk and repetitive framing boxes. | Consolidating 4 redundant epistemic disclaimer boxes and repetitive inline `"سناریو v6.4 (غیرثبتی)"` strings into a single structured **Epistemic Trust Pill** + expandable 5-layer provenance table. | Legal and epistemic honesty requires that the distinction between *Asking Price (`نرخ آگهی`)* and *Model Scenario Deal Price (`سناریوی معامله`)* never be hidden completely. |
| **R-11** | **Ben Shneiderman** (1996) — *Visual Information-Seeking Mantra ("Overview first, zoom and filter, then details-on-demand")* | Spatial and analytical systems must provide a clean macro overview first, fast filtering second, and deep item inspection on demand. | Restores the Map view as a clean spatial overview (`>= 72%` unobstructed on `390×844`), places budget/green-only filtering in the compact top strip, and delivers neighborhood details via the 3-state bottom sheet. | First-time home buyers with constrained budgets (`3.5B` Toman) often need guided parameter filtering *before* the map overview is meaningful (`ASSESS-003`). |
| **R-12** | **W3C Internationalization (i18n) & Persian Typography (`Vazirmatn` / Unicode UAX #9 Bidirectional Algorithm)** | Mixed RTL Persian text and LTR numeric/Latin tokens require explicit directional isolation (`dir="ltr"`, `unicode-bidi: isolate`) and tabular numerals (`tnum`). | Enforces `tabular-nums` across all Toman/m² price cards, comparison matrices, and slider readouts, and fixes bidirectional parenthesis/unit ordering in provenance badges. | Users may switch between `IRT` (Persian numerals in millions/billions) and `USD/EUR` (LTR decimal formatting), requiring unit-aware directional wrappers. |
