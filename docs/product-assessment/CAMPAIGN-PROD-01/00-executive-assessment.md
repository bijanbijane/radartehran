## 1. Executive Summary

Radar Tehran has achieved high engineering maturity across its backend data pipelines, hedonic valuation engine (`src/data/hedonicPricingEngine.ts`), multidimensional market price aggregation (`src/utils/realMarketPriceAggregator.ts`), and 159-neighborhood multi-city dataset (`Tehran`, `Karaj`, `Mashhad`, `Isfahan`, `Shiraz`, `Tabriz`). However, **live runtime inspection across 9 viewports (`320×568` to `1440×900`) and full AST/source reconstruction reveal that the human-facing product experience suffers from severe spatial compression on mobile, unconstrained card stacking in the Neighborhood Inspector, and fragmented, contradictory budget onboarding surfaces.**

Rather than lacking features, the interface suffers from **un-orchestrated feature accumulation**:
1. **Mobile Map Suffocation (`ASSESS-001`)**: Five simultaneous fixed/floating chrome layers (`ProductGlobalHeader`, `FloatingTopBar`, `MapLegend`, collapsed `NeighborhoodInspectorDrawer`, and 7-tab `RadarBottomNav`) leave only **`132px` (23%)** of unobstructed map height on `320×568` and **`448px` (53%)** on `390×844`. Worse, the bottom sheet has **zero touch-drag physics** (`mobileSheetOpen` is a binary tap toggle between `78px` and `78vh`), CSS ergonomic classes in `src/styles/tokens.css` (`.radar-drawer-scroll-container`, `.radar-drawer-drag-handle`) are **orphaned and never applied in JSX**, and `FloatingTopBar` (`z-40`) visually overlaps the top `40px` of the expanded bottom sheet (`z-30`).
2. **Inspector Above-the-Fold Starvation & Nested Tab Maze (`ASSESS-002`)**: On a `390×844` mobile screen, opening the Neighborhood Inspector displays **zero primary price cards and zero inspector tabs above the fold**—the entire initial viewport is consumed by an `88px` bloated header (repeating global unit/language/currency switches), a shortlist chip bar, stacked provenance boxes, and `QuickHoodRankingStrip`. Beneath the fold, the `scorecard` tab alone stacks **11 analytical cards**, followed by 6 global CTA buttons, a photo gallery, and an **open-by-default `<details open>` Stage-2 Master Widget containing 4 domains and 12 additional tabs**, resulting in over **6.5–8.0 screens of vertical scroll** inside a narrow `390px`/`396px` container.
3. **Onboarding Fragmentation & Semantic Contradictions (`ASSESS-003`)**: First-time visitors land on a default state of **`mode = 'rent'`** (2.8B deposit + 45M rent) and **`buyTotalBudget = 24,500M` (24.5 Billion Toman)**, with `showOnboardingModal` hardcoded to `false` on startup (`tehran_radar_onboarded_v1` is written on close but never read on mount). A first-time buyer with **3.5 Billion Toman (`3,500M`) and `75m²` target area** encounters **four competing budget entry surfaces** with inconsistent ranges, presets (`5B+` minimum preset), and missing financial levers (`reverse_budget` modal lacks the `+1.2B` mortgage and tenant *rahn* toggles present in `OnboardingWizardModal`). Most critically, on `ProductHomeView`, `BudgetFitVerdict.tsx` restricts evaluation to 20 central Tehran neighborhoods (ignoring the 5 matching Tehran Province areas like `پرند` at `3.15B`) and renders the warning badge **`"جا نمی‌شود"` (Does Not Fit) in emerald green (`text-emerald-800 dark:text-emerald-300`)**, violating semantic color trust.

## 3. Recommended Target Architecture (Hybrid Option A + Option C)

Based on the 15-criteria weighted decision matrix (`05-design-alternatives-and-decision-matrix.md`), we recommend a **Spatial-First Progressive Disclosure Architecture (Hybrid of Option A Evolutionary Refinement + Option C Task-Oriented Workspace)** that strictly respects all `ARCHITECTURE.md` guardrails (`< 500` lines per file, unidirectional dependency graph, zero hedonic engine regression):

1. **Unified Mobile Chrome & 3-State Gesture Bottom Sheet (`ASSESS-001` Fix)**:
   - Suppress duplicate city/search/budget rows in `FloatingTopBar` on `< 768px` (`ProductGlobalHeader` owns global search/city/budget pill; a compact floating map pill bar provides quick filter chips), restoring **>= 72% unobstructed map canvas** on `390×844` and **>= 64%** on `320×568`.
   - Upgrade `NeighborhoodInspectorDrawer` to a deterministic **3-state state machine** (`collapsed: 72px`, `peek: 38dvh`, `expanded: 86dvh`) with both pointer-capture drag (`onPointerDown`/`Move`/`Up` + velocity snap) and accessible 1-tap cycle buttons, wired to `.radar-drawer-scroll-container` and `.radar-drawer-drag-handle`.
   - Consolidate `RadarBottomNav` from 7 cramped columns to **4 primary destinations + 1 "بیشتر" (`More`) sheet** (`5` columns, `>= 64px` width on `320px`, `>= 48px` height), and enforce `>= 44×44px` touch targets on all mobile controls.
   - Default `splitMapMode` to `false` on mobile (`< 1024px`) for non-map views (`discover`, `intelligence`, `tools`, `userspace`), replacing the unusable `136px` map sliver with a sticky `"مشاهده روی نقشه"` floating pill.
2. **3-Tier Progressive Inspector Information Architecture (`ASSESS-002` Fix)**:
   - **Tier 1 (Above-the-Fold Sticky Summary — visible in `peek` & `expanded`)**: Neighborhood Title + 1-line compact Epistemic Trust Pill (`اعتماد داده: ۷۸٪ · نمونه بازار: ۲۴ آگهی`) + 2-column Buy/Rent Price Card + 1-line Budget Fit Verdict + 4-tab bar (`کارنامه`, `بازار و روند`, `معاملات و شکار`, `دیدگاه و میدان`).
   - **Tier 2 (Tab-Scoped Analytical Cards — max 4 cards visible by default per tab)**: Relocate `QuickHoodRankingStrip` out of the global drawer header into the `کارنامه` tab or a collapsible drawer switcher; move `SmartPriceForecastCard` and `NeighborhoodMonthlyBarChart` inside the `market` tab above global CTAs.
   - **Tier 3 (On-Demand Deep Dives)**: Set `Stage2MasterWidget` `<details>` to **closed by default (`open={false}`)** on mobile and standard mode (`uiMode === 'simple'`), opening automatically only in `Pro` mode or on explicit user tap.
3. **Unified First-Time Buyer Onboarding & Cross-City Affordability Bridge (`ASSESS-003` Fix)**:
   - Consolidate the 4 fragmented budget modals into a single canonical **Unified Budget & Purchasing Power Engine** shared by `OnboardingWizardModal` and `reverse_budget`, featuring realistic entry-level presets (`2.5B`, `3.5B`, `5B`, `8B`, `12B`, `20B`), unified loan (`+1.2B`) & tenant *rahn* levers, and a **1-Tap Cross-City Affordability Bridge** ("با بودجه ۳.۵ میلیارد تومان، ۵ محدوده در استان تهران و ۷ محله در کرج در دسترس شماست — مقایسه فوری").
   - Fix `BudgetFitVerdict.tsx` semantic color bug (`"جا نمی‌شود"` must use `amber/rose` tokens, never `emerald`) and evaluate across all city/province neighborhoods rather than only 20 central hoods.
